Lore Nobody Requested — 01

Brb.. I need to ask my mom if I can move in-

This is what Adam said: “Up until now, there’s nothing that seems concerning, I just need to chat with my mom before giving you a response.”

A sentence that Eve stopped at.

She tried to rationalize it away, then firmly said that she wouldn’t move in with someone who needed his momma’s approval.

A moment of awkward silence before Adam said that his parents had more experience than him, so they’d know how to file taxes as a couple.

Against all odds, Eve tried to follow his rationale: “But neither of your parents is an accountant, and you have your own accountant who files your taxes, whom you can ask.”

Adam stuck to his reasoning: “But they have more experience in general.”

Eve actually knew way sooner that she was dating someone who had a complicated attachment to his mom, with whom she’d have probably ended up being compared to. But in this moment, she decided to break up.

As she approached that conversation, Adam pushed back that she had already said it was fine if they remained a couple and lived in different places.

“I said that if we’re not ready to move in, then we’d do it when we’re ready, but I never said that the shape of the relationship I want to be in is living separately in the long term.”

To which Adam said that, oh, it was his bad. He misunderstood.

“I need to chat with my mom about her needing to be less involved in my sister’s life so that she wouldn’t end up being broken up with.” Adam said as he apologized for not being able to make decisions on his own.

As she tried repairing what was (or maybe wasn't there), "what if your mom said that it was the worst decision you'd ever make? what would your response be?" Adam seemed to have forgotten how to speak in this moment as Eve could feel her heart getting heavier and heavier.

You’d think that Eve would back off from someone who seemed to have minimal autonomy outside of his relationship with his parent.

But she chose to try and “Jesus” the relationship.
